# ADR-002 Four Channels

## Decision

Provide 4 channels for each I/O family.

## Motivation

Four channels are a good compromise between cost, front-panel / PCB size, and practical versatility for laboratory testing.

## Consequences

- Useful multi-channel bring-up scenarios
- Manageable panel density and BOM growth
- Homogeneous channel replication across AO, AI, RO, DI, DO, and contacts
